# Research and evidence

Distinguish primary research, summarized outside evidence, standards, expert interpretation and synthetic examples. Do not invent studies or claim findings apply outside their observed populations.

- [Research methods](../knowledge/guidance/ux-research-methods.md)
- [Experiment design](../knowledge/guidance/experimentation-and-causal-evidence.md)
- [Reference register](../governance/references.md)

This is an evidence **entry point**. Future real study and source-review records should be added with dates, methods, sample, limits, citation/license, owner, and reviewer. Until then, no studies are implied.
